## Capitalization Policy

- **De minimis safe harbor threshold:** $2,500.00 per item. Purchases below
  the threshold are expensed in the current year; at or above, capitalized.
- **Capitalize to basis:** inspection/appraisal tied to the purchased
  property, title company fees, escrow/closing fees, recording fees,
  transfer/documentary/filing fees, FF&E conveyed at closing.
- **Expense in current year:** normal utilities, routine repairs and
  maintenance below the threshold.

## Revenue Recognition (Airbnb)

Rental income is recognized on an **accrual basis at guest checkout** (the
reservation End date), not at payout. Airbnb service fees are booked as a
property management expense on the payout date. Occupancy taxes collected by
the platform are pass-through and excluded from income.
