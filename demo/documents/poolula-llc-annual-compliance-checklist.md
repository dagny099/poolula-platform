> **Demo dataset notice:** This document is part of the Poolula Platform demo
> corpus. All dates, agencies, and identifiers are fictional or generic.

## State & Entity Compliance

- [ ] **Colorado Periodic Report** — file annually with the Colorado Secretary
  of State by the anniversary month of formation (May). $10 filing fee.
  Required to maintain good standing.
- [ ] **Registered agent** — confirm Peakview Registered Agents LLC engagement
  is current and paid.
- [ ] **Annual member meeting** — hold and document minutes per the Operating
  Agreement (no statutory date required in Colorado).

## Federal & State Tax

- [ ] **Quarterly estimated tax payments** — April 15, June 15, September 15,
  and January 15 of the following year, if annual liability exceeds $1,000.
- [ ] **Schedule E (Form 1040)** — rental income reported on the member's
  individual return, due April 15 (extension to October 15 with Form 4868).
- [ ] **Depreciation schedule** — update the 27.5-year residential rental
  schedule for Building & Improvements (placed in service January 15, 2025).

## County & Local

- [ ] **Property tax, first half** — due February 28 to the Kestrel County
  Treasurer.
- [ ] **Property tax, second half** — due June 15 to the Kestrel County
  Treasurer.
- [ ] **Lodging / occupancy tax** — remit occupancy taxes collected on
  short-term stays per county schedule (license number pending — see
  gaps_to_fill).
